The question

We began by asking what mobility does to urban life: to belonging, relationships, political community and the ways people imagine their futures.

The project changed as it unfolded. Methods shifted. COVID intervened. The cities repeatedly resisted the neat containers we brought to them.

Eight years later, a simpler question remained: can an urban life really be contained by the city in which it is lived?
